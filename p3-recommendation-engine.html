What the animation means

  • 1Your typed movie becomes an edge from You to that movie.
  • 2The canvas first checks displayed users, then fills the rest with random MovieLens users who rated it at least 3.5 stars.
  • 3Each P3 walk samples one of your movies, one displayed user linked to it, then one other movie that user liked.

Dataset: MovieLens ml-latest-small, generated 2018-09-26. User ids are anonymized. This demo uses ratings of 3.5 or higher as liked edges.
